# Testing

Wearable Lanterns Canary 2 (`0.1.0-canary2`) is implemented and retained but has not been deployed or formally runtime-tested. External Canary 1 spot feedback reported solid core wearable behavior, a mismatched empty-slot sprite color, and visibly choppier worn-light movement than a held light; that feedback guides this procedure but is not a dedicated Workbench result.

## Canary 2 runtime acceptance procedure

1. Deploy the exact retained Canary 2 JAR only through a later authorized Test Instance Manager transition. Confirm its filename and SHA-256 before launch, and record the exact Test Slot and accepted-baseline identities.
2. Open the survival inventory and confirm the corrected empty `legs/lantern` sprite retains the Canary 1 lantern glyph, position, and transparency while matching the established Matcha GUI palette: highlight `#7F664D`, body `#604D3A`, shadow `#291F18`, and black interior.
3. With the ordinary belt slot assigned, confirm native Trinkets layout reveals lantern-left, leggings-center, and belt-right. Record the belt-absent native result separately; Canary 2 adds no custom GUI/layout mixin.
4. Directly equip and unequip one regular lantern and one soul lantern. Confirm amount one, exact eligibility, invalid-item rejection, independent belt contents, and no loss or duplication.
5. Repeat representative direct and shift-click transfers from Inventory Extended's upper rows, row six, hotbar, and creative inventory. Confirm the accepted dynamic menu-boundary behavior, native wrappers, storage-before-hotbar return routing, stack remainders, and absence of ghosts or fixed-index failures.
6. Relog, restart, and change dimensions; test death with keepInventory both disabled and enabled. Confirm normal Trinkets persistence, synchronization, drop/keep behavior, and belt independence for the local player and a representative observed remote player where available.
7. In third-person, inventory preview, and another-player view, confirm the actual equipped ItemStack model renders at the intended hip attachment and follows resource-pack model/texture overrides. Cover representative standing, walking, sprinting, crouching, swimming, elytra, armor, and Fresh Animations/entity-model cases.
8. Before comparing dynamic-light motion, record every relevant LambDynamicLights 4.12.2+26.2 setting and keep it identical across cases, including dynamic-light mode, self-lighting, adaptive/background behavior, distance thresholds, and chunk-rebuild scheduler.
9. Using the same player, route, camera, terrain, frame-rate conditions, and LDL settings, compare a regular lantern held in the main hand with that same lantern equipped in exact `legs/lantern`. Walk, sprint, jump, and turn for each case. Repeat with a held torch as a movement-cadence reference while distinguishing its normal luminance 14 from the regular lantern's 15.
10. Confirm a regular lantern in `legs/lantern` resolves through LDL's current item manager to luminance 15. Confirm a soul lantern and at least one supported data-extended lantern retain their own item/block-derived luminance rather than being forced to 15.
11. Equip and unequip repeatedly while observing LDL's source/debug information where available. Light must appear and disappear promptly, source count must not double, and no duplicate, stale, or stuck source may remain after unequip, death, slot removal, relog, or dimension change.
12. Repeat the motion comparison with another nearby player wearing the lantern to distinguish local-camera perception from remote entity/chunk rebuild behavior. Record whether any differential remains under truly identical settings rather than inferring a Wearable Lanterns cause.
13. Verify the light is client-rendered only: no lantern block, light block, changed block state, server luminance, persistent environmental light, or world mutation may be created.
14. Remove LambDynamicLights and repeat representative equip, persistence, synchronization, and worn-render checks. Wearable Lanterns must load and function normally without errors or missing-class linkage.

Stop and preserve the exact state and logs on any crash, disconnect, item loss or duplication, invalid-item acceptance, belt/lantern collision, fixed-index failure, creative ghost slot, persistence or synchronization failure, duplicate or stuck dynamic-light source, world/block-light mutation, or material rendering regression. Treat a remaining held-versus-worn smoothness difference as unresolved evidence for LDL/configuration/renderer investigation; do not add a second player light source without a demonstrated distinct failing path.
