# Testing

Candidate `villager-work-routines-0.1.0-canary11.jar` is a user-managed Minecraft Java 26.2/Fabric runtime trial. Before testing, verify its version, filename, SHA-256, and source checkpoint against `WORKBENCH_STATUS.json`. Use the normal frog-villager resource pack, do not install the Real Working Villagers reference JAR, and retain relevant `[VillagerWorkRoutines]` logs. A static build/test pass is not runtime evidence.

## Recorded Canary 10 diagnostic evidence

Canary 10 loaded and ran without a VWR warning, error, or mixin failure. The user reported that the Fisherman looked better; the supplied log contains 47 complete fish result/deposit cycles with no retained output, but it does not establish the required visual endpoint/stick/cue checks and therefore is not a Fisherman PASS. The same log records nine successful Shepherd shears but only eight outward crossings and deposits. On the ninth cycle the Shepherd captured two wool, opened the known exit gate, timed out in `CROSS_EXIT`, closed the gate without a confirmed outward crossing, never deposited those two wool, then lost its claimed loom memory and emitted no further Shepherd activity while gameplay continued.

The log also records `The game is sprinting` before this sequence and no return to normal rate, so the result is formally **INCONCLUSIVE** as a Canary 10 acceptance trial rather than a compliant 20-TPS FAIL. It nevertheless exposed the deterministic crossing-boundary/route-loss defect corrected by Canary 11. The retained C5 Shepherd PASS remains historical evidence; Canary 11 requires a new normal-rate regression trial.

## Canary 11 client-start, Fisherman, and Shepherd recovery trial

Run visual and behavioral checks at normal **20 TPS**. Accelerated ticks may reach vanilla WORK time only; restore 20 TPS before navigation, casting, rendering, retrieval, or observation.

1. Start Minecraft and reach the main menu / normal client initialization. There must be no VWR mixin or `EntityRenderers` failure.
2. Confirm resource packs remain enabled; no VWR-caused resource-pack rollback may occur.
3. Enter a world with the normal frog-villager resource pack.
4. Confirm ordinary villagers render normally.
5. Give a Fisherman its own genuinely claimed barrel, valid bounded open source water, and a safe adjacent bank. Once the bobber appears, confirm one persistent plain stick at the crossed hands, shifted forward so it emerges past the arm area; it must have no duplicate vanilla line/hook or generic crossed-arms item. Confirm the line begins exactly at the stick tip and ends at the top of the bobber, without a visible gap, then confirm the stick disappears after retrieval or interruption.
6. Complete at least one Fisherman catch/deposit cycle: water/bank selection, navigation, cast, wait, retrieve/swing/splash, one fish-only result, owned-output capture, return, and deposit only to the claimed barrel. On a successful transfer, the Fisherman must face and swing toward that barrel while a barrel-open sound and a small burst of particles play at it; no cue may occur when nothing was inserted.
7. While the bobber is live, confirm the Fisherman remains at its vetted bank and keeps looking toward the water until retrieval; it must not resume ordinary wandering. Cause an ordinary world save/pause if practical. There must be no crash, lost float ownership, reset catch timing, or hand-state corruption. Then observe several cycles and an interruption; float, stick, and line must clean up once.
8. Give a Shepherd a genuinely claimed loom, a face-adjacent output barrel with capacity, and at least three adult regrown sheep behind a closed fence gate. At normal 20 TPS, observe at least ten complete cycles: target selection, correct gate, blocker activation, entry, shears telegraph, actual shear, exact wool capture, return through the same gate, safe closure/blocker release, and deposit. The sheep must remain contained.
9. During one outward crossing, if practical, briefly obstruct or nudge the outside landing so the Shepherd does not arrive at the exact adjacent-block center; do not leave another entity in the gate collision plane. Remove the obstruction and confirm the Shepherd either recognizes that it is already clear outside or retains/retries the known exit. A timeout must log `side`, position, and recovery; it must not discard the route, close the Shepherd inside, lose the captured wool, or stop permanently. If WORK ends or the loom claim disappears mid-crossing, safety cleanup must still finish the already-started exit.

Record **FAIL** for any startup/client crash, mixin application failure, `EntityRenderers` failure, VWR-caused resource-pack rollback, missing/duplicate/stale stick, a stick with a duplicate vanilla line/hook, a visible line-to-stick/bobber gap, moving/wandering during a live cast, absent/false barrel-deposit cue, fishing regression, line-geometry crash, legitimate-hand-state corruption, discarded exit route before a confirmed outside position, gate closure with the Shepherd still inside, lost captured wool, livestock escape, or other Shepherd regression. Record **INCONCLUSIVE** only with the observed limiting condition. Do not mark Canary 11 Fisherman or Shepherd PASS until a user observes the complete respective trial at normal 20 TPS.
