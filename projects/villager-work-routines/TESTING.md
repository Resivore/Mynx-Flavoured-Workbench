# Testing

Candidate `villager-work-routines-0.1.0-canary4.jar` is a user-managed runtime trial; a clean build does not establish a Minecraft runtime PASS. Before installation, verify its filename and SHA-256 against the current `WORKBENCH_STATUS.json`. Enable the user's frog-villager resource pack, and do not install the Real Working Villagers reference JAR alongside it. Save visible observations and matching `[VillagerWorkRoutines]` log lines with the exact candidate identity. Report Shepherd and Fisherman results independently; the Canary 3 report supplied no Fisherman verdict.

Run visual checks at normal **20 TPS**. A high `/tick rate` may be used only to advance to vanilla WORK time; restore 20 TPS before judging walking, gate opening/crossing/closing, shears presentation, swing, or shearing timing. Do not change gameplay timing to make a high tick rate visually observable.

1. **A — Open terrain, direct route.** Give one Shepherd a genuinely claimed loom and an adult unsheared sheep within the existing 12-block scan radius, with no enclosure. Start far enough away to see travel. During vanilla WORK, the Shepherd must detect and select the known sheep, use an ordinary direct path without gate search or gate changes, physically arrive on safe supported dry footing within 2.4 blocks with a clear shear line, visibly hold temporary shears for about 14 ticks, then swing and perform the real shear. Capture route endpoint and selection diagnostics, shears/action timing, exact wool capture, return to loom, and deposit into a face-adjacent barrel. The sheep must remain unsheared until arrival and clear-line validation.
2. **B — Normal closed sheep pen, entry and exit.** Put one eligible sheep inside an ordinary fence pen with one closed fence gate that is its actual entrance; place the claimed loom and Shepherd outside. Confirm the sheep is detected and selected **before** the Shepherd enters. The direct route should fail at the barrier, then diagnostics should identify that same sheep, its valid interaction position, the relevant gate and orientation, reachable near side, far side, and verified route onward to the sheep. Watch the full sequence at 20 TPS: approach gate, open it, cross fully, safely close it behind, continue to the retained sheep, show shears, shear, retain owned wool, return to the same gate, open it, cross outward, safely close it, return to loom, and deposit. Capture stage transitions and any rejection or timeout reasons. No shearing through the fence, teleporting, clipping, or permanent trapping is acceptable.
3. **C — Irrelevant second gate and multiple routes.** Add a nearer gate into a separate pen or dead end. Keep the selected sheep in the original pen. The Shepherd must not open the unrelated gate; the chosen gate must actually provide a route from its reachable side to a valid interaction position for that exact sheep. Where two gates genuinely reach the selected sheep, verify a deterministic sensible choice. Record gate candidates and rejection reasons without expecting per-tick route dumps.
4. **D — Already-open gate.** Start with the target pen's gate open. The Shepherd may take the available direct route or use that opening in its staged route; it must not claim to have opened the gate or close a gate it found already open. If another actor changes a gate during the routine, the Shepherd must reevaluate without repeatedly toggling against that actor. A redstone-powered open gate must not be forcibly closed.
5. **E — Pen with no entrance.** Put an eligible sheep behind a complete fence or wall with no gate or opening. It may be detected, but no legitimate gate route should be selected. The Shepherd must reject it as unreachable, leave the barrier intact, and never shear from the far side. Log the blocked direct endpoint, `barrierToSheep` result, and gate-search outcome.
6. **F — Gate interruption and cleanup.** If practical, interrupt a gate stage by ending WORK, trading, sleep, breeding, changing profession/job site, moving or shearing the target sheep, removing/replacing the gate, or blocking the passage during crossing. The routine must stop safely, clear transient route and synthetic tool state, avoid fighting external gate changes, and make only a safe best effort to close a gate it itself opened from closed. It must not close while an entity occupies the passage or while powered. Record the precise cancellation/closure reason and verify later WORK can resume. Save/reload, death, and lightning conversion must not leave synthetic shears as real inventory or drops.
7. **Eligibility and sheep selection.** In uninterrupted WORK near the player, eligibility must not rapidly alternate eligible/cancelled merely because `INTERACTION_TARGET` is present. Verify genuine cancellations state their cause: trading, sleep, breeding, WORK ending, profession change, lost/reassigned claimed loom, wrong dimension, or workstation block/POI mismatch. Babies and already-sheared sheep must be ignored; natural grass regrowth should make an adult eligible again. With several eligible sheep, selection should remain deterministic and bounded. Do not expect the Shepherd to wander through arbitrary gates to discover sheep.
8. **Shepherd ownership and storage.** Drop player-owned wool and unrelated items by the sheep; none may be vacuumed. Only the exact output of the Shepherd's own successful shear may enter persisted owned storage, preserving wool color/components. Place barrels on the loom's faces and an unrelated barrel elsewhere: only face-adjacent barrels may receive wool. Check matching stacks before empty slots, deterministic insertion, separation of component-mismatched stacks, and unchanged unrelated contents. Fill adjacent barrels: no overwrite, deletion, extraction, rearrangement, or fallback search; wool remains owned and new shearing pauses until capacity returns.
9. **Fisherman unchanged trial.** Separately give a Fisherman a genuinely claimed barrel and open village water within eight blocks with walkable solid shore. During WORK, observe shoreline/standing/path diagnostics, travel, rod, cast, isolated float/line, splash/retrieve, fish-only catch, return, and deposit into the claimed barrel. Check capacity, no nearby item vacuum or unclaimed-barrel deposit, and removal of the claimed barrel or water mid-cast. Only cod, salmon, tropical fish, or pufferfish may be caught; no treasure, junk, or fishing XP. Do not infer a Fisherman PASS from the Shepherd trial.
10. **Ambient, vanilla, and rendering.** Armorer, Butcher/Matcha chef, Cartographer, Cleric, Fletcher, Leatherworker, Librarian, Mason, Toolsmith, and Weaponsmith should only look, swing, and play restrained work sound at their own claimed station; station inventories/blockstates stay unchanged. Farmer, Nitwit, and unemployed villagers remain vanilla. Check trading, profession acquisition, breeding/social behavior, and general fence-gate behavior outside the Shepherd routine. With the frog pack, inspect head look, arm swing, shears/rod placement, float/line origin, and workstation gestures at several angles. Synthetic props must clear after interruption/reload/conversion; owned output must persist or drop as intended.

Stop and record FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE for a known sheep behind a normal valid gate that remains unreachable; opening an irrelevant gate; failure to enter, exit, or safely restore a Shepherd-opened gate; unsafe closure; through-fence shear; missing or same-tick visible shears; unexplained path/eligibility diagnostics; ownership or storage violation; Fisherman travel/catch/deposit failure; synthetic tool persistence; workstation side effects; broken vanilla behavior; renderer conflict; or crash. Record exact observations rather than inferring unobserved stages.
