# Testing

Candidate `villager-work-routines-0.1.0-canary2.jar` is ACTIVE, not deployed by this project task, and runtime untested. Runtime deployment and testing are user-managed. Enable the user's frog-villager resource pack. Do not install the Real Working Villagers reference JAR alongside this candidate. Build/unit results are not Minecraft runtime acceptance. Capture the game log lines prefixed `[VillagerWorkRoutines]` with the observations below; they identify the villager, claimed site, scan rejections, selected target, path, action, output, deposit, or cancellation stage.

1. **Shepherd eligibility and physical action.** Give a shepherd a genuinely claimed loom and place an adult unsheared sheep in a pen enclosed by fences or another solid collision barrier. The shepherd must not shear it from outside or through the barrier; check the sheep stays unsheared and the log reports no reachable interaction position, a blocked interaction, or a failed path. Then make an entrance/legitimate route or place a reachable sheep nearby within 12 blocks of the loom. During normal WORK, observe the shepherd visibly approach a valid side of the sheep, stop and face it, hold temporary shears perceptibly before swinging, then shear. Confirm the log's selected target, path, arrival, shears-equipped, actual-shear, capture, and deposit stages match what was seen. A shear from outside the pen or without visible pre-action shears is FAIL even if wool arrives. Check a baby and an already-sheared adult are ignored; after natural grass regrowth the adult is eligible again. Remove/reassign the loom, move the sheep beyond the local bound, or end WORK time: the custom routine must cancel and vanilla villager behavior resume.
2. **Shepherd ownership and storage.** Drop wool and unrelated items as a player near the sheep; none may be vacuumed. Verify only output from that shepherd's own successful shear enters its owned output. Put barrels on each of the loom's six faces and a separate non-adjacent barrel elsewhere; only face-adjacent barrels may receive wool. Check exact wool color, partial matching stacks before empty slots, component-mismatched stacks kept separate, unrelated contents unchanged, and deterministic choice when multiple adjacent barrels exist. Fill every adjacent barrel: no overwrite, deletion, extraction, rearrangement, or fallback search; wool stays with the villager and shearing stops when safe output capacity is exhausted. Reopen a slot and verify later retry.
3. **Fisherman shoreline and cycle.** Give a fisherman a genuinely claimed barrel and ordinary open village water within eight blocks of it, with a solid shore block beside the water at the water block's height and walkable air above the shore. During WORK, verify the log finds open water, selects a supported reachable standing position above that shore, starts navigation, and records arrival. Observe the fisherman leave the barrel, walk to the bank, face the water, briefly hold a rod, swing/cast with visible float and line, then splash/retrieve and carry fish to the exact claimed barrel. Compare the log's float spawn, scheduled retrieval, fish result, owned-output insertion, return, and barrel deposit with the visible sequence. If the fisherman stays at the barrel, the scan's no-open-water, unsafe/unloaded, standing-geometry, support, body-collision, and path counts should explain why; no explanation is INCONCLUSIVE, not a timing excuse. Repeat catches and verify only cod, salmon, tropical fish, or pufferfish appear; no treasure, junk, or fishing XP orbs. Drop unrelated fish/items nearby: they must not be collected. An unclaimed nearby barrel must never receive catch. Check matching and empty slots, component matching, full claimed barrel unchanged, fish retained and new casts paused while output has no safe slot, and retry when space opens. Remove/reassign the claimed barrel or remove the water mid-cast: the routine and float must cancel without adopting another barrel.
4. **Ambient and vanilla regressions.** For Armorer, Butcher/Matcha chef, Cartographer, Cleric, Fletcher, Leatherworker, Librarian, Mason, Toolsmith, and Weaponsmith, verify each uses only its own claimed workstation for occasional look/swing/work sound. Snapshot smoker, blast furnace, brewing stand, cauldron, lectern, and other workstation inventories/blockstates before and after: no item, fuel, fluid, book, map, block, projectile, loot, or resource change is allowed. Farmer and Nitwit remain vanilla. Verify trading, profession acquisition, breeding/social interactions, and normal fence-gate behavior remain intact.
5. **Prop and frog-pack rendering.** While working and after interruption, save/reload, profession change, death, and lightning conversion, check shears/rod never persist or become loot; real carried wool/fish remains available or drops on death/conversion. With the frog pack, inspect head look, 3 px arm swings, shears and rod placement, float and line origin, and workstation gestures at several view angles. Report cosmetic offsets without changing villager geometry.

Stop and record FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE on shearing through a barrier, invisible or same-tick shears, failure to accept a reachable ordinary shore, failure to travel/cast/retrieve/deposit, unexplained diagnostic stage, resource ownership/storage violation, synthetic tool loot/persistence, workstation side effect, non-fish catch or XP, broken vanilla behavior, renderer conflict, or crash. Record the exact Canary 2 release filename/SHA-256, log stage lines, and observed cases before any acceptance decision.
