# Testing

Candidate `villager-work-routines-0.1.0-canary18.jar` is a user-managed Minecraft Java 26.2/Fabric runtime trial. Before testing, verify its version, filename, SHA-256, and source checkpoint against `WORKBENCH_STATUS.json`. For the authored-rod path, also verify the paired Ribbits Canary 28 identity from its own `WORKBENCH_STATUS.json`. Use the normal frog-villager resource pack, do not install the Real Working Villagers reference JAR, and retain relevant `[VillagerWorkRoutines]` logs. A static build/test pass is not runtime evidence.

## Canary 18 Ribbits Fisherman rod bridge and regression trial

Run visual and behavioral checks at normal **20 TPS**. Accelerated ticks may reach vanilla WORK time only; restore 20 TPS before navigation, casting, rendering, retrieval, or observation.

1. Start Minecraft and reach the main menu / normal client initialization. There must be no VWR mixin or `EntityRenderers` failure.
2. Confirm resource packs remain enabled; no VWR-caused resource-pack rollback may occur.
3. Enter a world with the normal frog-villager resource pack.
4. Confirm ordinary villagers render normally.
5. Give a Fisherman its own genuinely claimed barrel, valid bounded open source water, and a safe adjacent bank. With the paired Ribbits Canary 28 visual provider installed, once the VWR bobber appears, confirm one persistent cute Ribbits Fisherman rod at the crossed hands in C17's established lower/inward general presentation. It must use only the authored `fishing_rod` shaft/reel/detail: no Ribbits decorative hanging second line or decorative terminal bobber may appear, and there must be no duplicate vanilla line/hook or generic crossed-arms item. Confirm VWR's real 16-segment line visibly begins at the actual visible outer rod tip with no gap, then reaches only the existing VWR float. The established cast, line shape, float, and general presentation must otherwise remain unchanged. Confirm rod and line clean up once after retrieval or interruption. Then make the Ribbits provider/resources unavailable in a separate user-managed launch and repeat one cast: VWR must safely retain its C17 plain-stick fallback and existing line/float behavior without a client failure.
6. Complete at least one Fisherman catch/deposit cycle: water/bank selection, navigation, cast, wait, retrieve/swing/splash, one fish-only result, owned-output capture, return, and deposit only to the claimed barrel. On a successful transfer, the Fisherman must face and swing toward that barrel while the barrel-open sound and existing glow-squid-ink burst occur immediately, followed about six ticks later by exactly one modestly louder cod-flop cue. No cue may occur when nothing was inserted or when a queued successful transfer is cancelled before its delayed moment.
7. While the bobber is live, confirm the Fisherman remains at its vetted bank and keeps looking toward the water until retrieval; it must not resume ordinary wandering. Cause an ordinary world save/pause if practical. There must be no crash, lost float ownership, reset catch timing, or hand-state corruption. Then observe several cycles and an interruption; VWR float, rod (or fallback stick), and line must clean up once.
8. Give a Shepherd a genuinely claimed loom, a face-adjacent output barrel with capacity, and at least three adult regrown sheep behind a closed fence gate. At normal 20 TPS, observe at least ten complete cycles: target selection (shears should appear immediately and persist through approach, gate traversal, telegraph, shear, and brief cleanup), correct gate, blocker activation, entry, actual shear, exact wool capture, return through the same gate, safe closure/blocker release, and deposit. Each barrel that actually accepts wool must receive the Shepherd's look/swing and barrel-open sound immediately, then after a brief deliberate pause one wool-place sound synchronized with a modest, clearly visible END_ROD burst around that exact barrel. Confirm neither delayed cue appears for a full barrel, failed transfer, or cancelled attempt. The sheep must remain contained.
9. During one outward crossing, if practical, briefly obstruct or nudge the outside landing so the Shepherd does not arrive at the exact adjacent-block center; do not leave another entity in the gate collision plane. Remove the obstruction and confirm the Shepherd either recognizes that it is already clear outside or retains/retries the known exit. A timeout must log `side`, position, and recovery; it must not discard the route, close the Shepherd inside, lose the captured wool, or stop permanently. If WORK ends or the loom claim disappears mid-crossing, safety cleanup must still finish the already-started exit.

Record **FAIL** for any startup/client crash, mixin application failure, `EntityRenderers` failure, VWR-caused resource-pack rollback, missing/duplicate/stale Ribbits rod, fallback stick, or shears; any visible Ribbits decorative line/bobber; a rod or fallback stick with a duplicate vanilla line/hook; a visible line-to-rod-tip/float gap; a provider-path rod that no longer retains the established C17 general placement; moving/wandering during a live cast; a Shepherd whose shears disappear during an active transaction; absent/false barrel-deposit cue; duplicate or immediate Fisherman cod-flop; fishing regression; line-geometry crash; legitimate-hand-state corruption; discarded exit route before a confirmed outside position; gate closure with the Shepherd still inside; lost captured wool; livestock escape; or other Shepherd regression. Record **INCONCLUSIVE** only with the observed limiting condition. Do not mark Canary 18 Fisherman or Shepherd PASS until a user observes the complete respective trial at normal 20 TPS.
