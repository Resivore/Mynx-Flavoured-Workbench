# Testing

Candidate `villager-work-routines-0.1.0-canary5.jar` is a user-managed Minecraft runtime trial. Before installing it, verify the exact version, filename, SHA-256, and source checkpoint against the current `WORKBENCH_STATUS.json`; a clean build or static test pass is not runtime evidence. Enable the user's frog-villager resource pack, do not install the Real Working Villagers reference JAR, and preserve matching `[VillagerWorkRoutines]` log lines with each observation. Canary 4 supplied no Fisherman verdict, so report Shepherd and Fisherman results independently.

Run behavioral and visual checks at normal **20 TPS**. Accelerated ticks may be used only to reach vanilla WORK time; restore 20 TPS before judging walking, temporary props, gate opening/crossing/closing, animal movement, swinging, shearing, casting, or retrieval.

1. **A — Shepherd and a normal closed sheep pen.** Give a Shepherd a genuinely claimed loom with a deterministic face-adjacent output barrel. Put an adult unsheared sheep inside an ordinary fence pen with one initially closed gate and begin with the Shepherd outside. Confirm it detects and retains the sheep before gate discovery, rejects the blocked direct path, selects only the gate that connects toward that same sheep, approaches it, activates the livestock blocker as it opens the gate, immediately crosses, and closes promptly once safely clear. It must continue to the known sheep without rescanning the gate, approach with visible movement margin, face it, show temporary shears even if the normal hand was occupied, tolerate or recover from small sheep movement, complete the readable telegraph, pass final footing/distance/clear-line/shearability checks, swing, perform the real shear, capture the exact generated wool, and restore the legitimate hand state. It must then return through the same gate with blocker coverage over the complete owned-open interval, close promptly after outward crossing, return to the loom, and deposit into the face-adjacent barrel. Sheep must not deliberately path out through either owned-open interval, and through-fence shearing, teleporting, clipping, or a lingering open gate is a failure.

2. **B — Five-species livestock containment.** Where practical, enclose sheep, cow, pig, chicken, and rabbit behind the Shepherd's closed gate and trigger both entry and exit. While VWR owns the physically open gate, none of those five species should choose a path through it; the Shepherd must remain able to traverse. Ordinary wandering remains enabled, and an animal being pushed or knocked physically through the opening is outside this feature's contract. Capture the single activation/release diagnostics and any rate-limited denial summary, not per-node path logs.

3. **C — Player-opened gate remains vanilla.** With no active VWR-owned transition, open the same gate manually. No blocker may register, VWR must not close or claim the gate, and livestock navigation through it must remain normal vanilla behavior.

4. **D — Gate already open before arrival.** Leave the gate open before the Shepherd starts. The Shepherd may use the existing passage, but must not claim, livestock-block, or close it merely because it traversed it. Livestock behavior remains vanilla. A powered or externally controlled gate must likewise not be fought.

5. **E — Moving sheep retains the task.** Let the selected sheep wander during final approach and the approximately 14-tick shears telegraph. Small movement inside the local pen should keep the same sheep UUID, cause a closer approach or telegraph restart only when needed, and eventually shear if final checks become valid. Confirm diagnostics distinguish movement, same-target repositioning, restart, final distance, and final clear-line result. Death, becoming a baby or already sheared, leaving the bounded area, output-capacity loss, genuine obstruction, or bounded unreachability may abandon the target; ordinary movement must not reopen or rescan the gate.

6. **F — Occupied hand transaction.** Reproduce a legitimate occupied Shepherd main hand if possible, for example by observing normal nearby-player trade-display behavior. VWR must preserve the original stack and drop state, present marked synthetic shears, withstand normal replacement/clear attempts without `main hand occupied` or `shears lost` cancellation, and restore the newest legitimate hand state on success or cancellation. A genuinely new external hand change must not be blindly overwritten. Verify no legitimate stack is deleted or duplicated, and no synthetic shears enter villager inventory, persist, or drop.

7. **G — No entrance.** Put an eligible sheep behind a complete fence or wall with no gate or opening. It may be detected, but no gate route should be selected and no barrier should change. The Shepherd must not teleport, clip, or shear through the enclosure.

8. **Interruptions, reload, and external changes.** During approach, entry, telegraph, return, and exit, selectively end WORK, trade, sleep, breed, change profession or claimed job site, shear/move/remove the target, power or close the gate externally, replace/remove the gate block, or obstruct the true closed-state gate collision. Cancellation must preserve the known exit obligation when necessary, keep blocker ownership aligned with the actual VWR-owned open interval, restore or safely relinquish the gate without stale registrations, clear/restore the temporary hand transaction, and later resume cleanly. Save/reload an open owned-gate exit obligation if practical: a legitimate resumed obligation must reconstruct blocker coverage before continuing, while stale ownership must not. Death, unload, and lightning conversion must not leave or drop synthetic props. VWR may defer closure while an entity genuinely intersects the closed gate shape, but proximity to the wider gate block must not leave it open unnecessarily.

9. **Shepherd ownership and storage.** Drop player-owned wool and unrelated items near the sheep; VWR must never vacuum them. Only output from its exact successful `Sheep.shear` call may enter persisted owned storage, preserving wool color and components. Only barrels on faces of the claimed loom are eligible, with deterministic matching-stack then empty-slot insertion, no overwrite, extraction, rearrangement, deletion, or nearest-container fallback. Fill all eligible capacity: retained wool must remain owned and further shearing must pause until capacity returns.

10. **Fisherman freeze and independent verdict.** Do not infer a Fisherman result from this Shepherd trial. If tested separately, use a genuinely claimed barrel and reachable open source water within eight blocks; verify shoreline selection, travel, temporary rod, cast, isolated float/line, splash/retrieve, fish-only capture, return, and claimed-barrel deposit. Only cod, salmon, tropical fish, or pufferfish may be retained; no treasure, junk, XP, nearby-item vacuum, or unclaimed-barrel deposit is allowed. Removing the claimed barrel or water must cancel safely, and shared temporary-hand cleanup must not otherwise redesign Fisherman behavior.

11. **Ambient and vanilla freeze.** Armorer, Butcher/Matcha chef, Cartographer, Cleric, Fletcher, Leatherworker, Librarian, Mason, Toolsmith, and Weaponsmith remain cosmetic claimed-workstation activity only: look, hand swing, and restrained work sound with no station inventory or block-state mutation. No butcher or rancher behavior is introduced. Farmer, Nitwit, and unemployed villagers remain vanilla. Trading, profession acquisition, breeding/social behavior, players, hostile mobs, and non-target passive mobs must be unaffected. With the frog pack, inspect head look, swing, shears/rod placement, float/line origin, and workstation gestures from several angles.

Expected C5 transition logs include target selection; final approach distance; original hand preservation; synthetic overlay start; telegraph start/restart; recoverable sheep movement and same-target repositioning; final distance and clear line; actual shear; wool capture; hand restoration; and livestock blocker activation/release with gate and owner. Do not expect logs for every tick, animal, or path node.

Stop and record **FAIL** or **INCONCLUSIVE** for failure to enter/exit through the correct gate, delayed or unsafe owned-gate closure, stale/missing blocker coverage, target-species intentional escape through a VWR-owned opening, any global effect on ordinary open gates, lost/duplicated hand state, synthetic prop persistence/drop, small-movement task abandonment, through-barrier shear, missing final validation, wool ownership/storage violation, Fisherman regression, ambient/vanilla behavior change, renderer conflict, crash, or diagnostics too ambiguous to bind the observation to the exact C5 candidate.
