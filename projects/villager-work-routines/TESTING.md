# Testing

Candidate `villager-work-routines-0.1.0-canary3.jar` is ACTIVE, not deployed by this project task, and runtime untested. Runtime deployment and testing are user-managed. Enable the user's frog-villager resource pack, and do not install the Real Working Villagers reference JAR alongside this candidate. A clean build is not a game-runtime PASS. Record the exact candidate filename/SHA-256, visible observations, and matching `[VillagerWorkRoutines]` log lines before any acceptance decision.

1. **Discover and select.** In a controlled open setup, give a Shepherd a genuinely claimed loom and place three nearby adult unsheared sheep within 12 blocks of it. During vanilla WORK, the scan should find all three, mark them eligible, and select at least one. Capture the selected sheep ID/position and route-attempt details: generated interaction positions, requested BlockPos, `createPath` result, `canReach`, reported target, actual endpoint, deltas, `moveTo` result, and rejection reason. An obviously reachable position must not produce unexplained `noPath`.
2. **Walk, arrive, and shear.** Start the Shepherd far enough away to see it navigate to the selected sheep. It must physically arrive at a collision-clear, supported, dry position, within 2.4 blocks of the sheep, with an unobstructed shear line. It must stop, face the sheep, visibly hold shears for the 14-tick telegraph, then swing and shear. The sheep must stay unsheared until arrival and clear-line validation. Compare selected/navigation, arrival/distance/clear-line, telegraph start, actual shear, wool captured, and deposit log stages. Synthetic shears should remain briefly after shearing and then disappear.
3. **Fence safety and legitimate route.** Place an eligible sheep behind a complete fence or wall with no traversable entrance. The Shepherd must decline it and never shear through the barrier. Route diagnostics should show the specific null path, unreachable or distant endpoint, or blocked endpoint shear line. Then provide a legitimate open route, such as an already-open fence gate, and verify the Shepherd walks through it normally before shearing from a valid position. This Canary does not open closed gates; a closed gate is a blocked route, not permission to shear through it.
4. **Eligibility transitions.** Keep the Shepherd in ordinary uninterrupted WORK near the creative player for several scan periods. Eligibility must not flip rapidly between eligible and cancelled. Every remaining cancellation must state the exact cause. Check genuine interruptions: player trading, sleep, breeding, end of WORK activity, profession change, lost or reassigned job site, wrong dimension, and workstation block/POI mismatch. The custom action should yield and later resume appropriately. Check that babies and already-sheared sheep are ignored and natural grass regrowth makes an adult eligible again.
5. **Shepherd ownership and storage.** Drop player-owned wool and unrelated items near the sheep; the Shepherd must not vacuum them. Only wool from its own successful shear may enter its owned output, preserving exact color and components. Put barrels on the loom's faces and another barrel elsewhere: only face-adjacent barrels may receive wool. Verify matching stacks before empty slots, deterministic order, component-mismatched stacks kept separate, and all unrelated contents unchanged. Fill the adjacent barrels: no overwrite, deletion, extraction, rearrangement, or fallback search; wool stays owned and further shearing pauses until capacity returns.
6. **Fisherman unchanged trial.** Separately give a Fisherman a genuinely claimed barrel and ordinary open village water within eight blocks, with walkable solid shore beside it. During WORK, compare C2 shoreline/standing/path diagnostics with visible travel, rod, cast, isolated float/line, splash/retrieve, fish-only catch, return, and deposit into the claimed barrel. Test output capacity, no nearby item vacuum, no unclaimed barrel deposit, and removal of the claimed barrel or water mid-cast. Only cod, salmon, tropical fish, or pufferfish should be caught; no treasure, junk, or fishing XP. The user's Canary 2 trial did not establish a Fisherman runtime result.
7. **Ambient and vanilla regressions.** Armorer, Butcher/Matcha chef, Cartographer, Cleric, Fletcher, Leatherworker, Librarian, Mason, Toolsmith, and Weaponsmith should only look/swing/play restrained work sound at their own claimed station. Their station inventories and blockstates must remain unchanged. Farmer, Nitwit, and unemployed villagers remain vanilla. Check trading, profession acquisition, breeding/social behavior, and general fence-gate behavior.
8. **Persistence and rendering.** After work interruption, save/reload, profession change, death, and lightning conversion, shears/rod must never persist as real inventory or drop; owned wool/fish remains available or drops appropriately. With the frog pack, inspect head look, arm swing, prop placement, float/line origin, and workstation gestures at several angles.

Stop and record FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE on shearing through a barrier, missing or same-tick visible shears, failure to select/walk to an obviously reachable sheep, unexplained path or eligibility diagnostics, ownership/storage violation, Fisherman travel/catch/deposit failure, synthetic tool persistence, workstation side effect, broken vanilla behavior, renderer conflict, or crash. Report Shepherd and Fisherman results independently.
