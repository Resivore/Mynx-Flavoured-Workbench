These are scoped C18/C28 observations only; they establish no aggregate PASS and no C19/C29 runtime result.

1. Start Minecraft and reach the main menu / normal client initialization. There must be no VWR mixin or `EntityRenderers` failure.
2. Confirm resource packs remain enabled; no VWR-caused resource-pack rollback may occur.
3. Enter a world with the normal frog-villager resource pack.
4. Confirm ordinary villagers render normally.
5. Give a Fisherman its own genuinely claimed barrel, valid bounded open source water, and a safe adjacent bank. With paired Ribbits Canary 29 installed, once the VWR bobber appears, confirm one persistent Ribbits Fisherman rod whose actual `fishing_rod` grip is held at the crossed hands and whose shaft follows a natural hand-held pose. Confirm VWR's real 16-segment line begins exactly at the visible outer shaft tip with no gap, ends only at the existing VWR float, and never derives a detached high world/view endpoint. Cast, line shape, float, and behavior must otherwise remain unchanged. Confirm rod and line clean up once after retrieval or interruption. Then make the Ribbits provider/resources unavailable in a separate user-managed launch and repeat one cast: VWR must safely retain its C17 plain-stick fallback and existing line/float behavior without a client failure.

Cause an ordinary world save/pause if practical. There must be no crash, lost float ownership, reset catch timing, or hand-state corruption. Confirm neither delayed cue appears for a full barrel, failed transfer, or cancelled attempt. The sheep must remain contained.
