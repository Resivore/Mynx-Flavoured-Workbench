# Testing

Candidate `villager-work-routines-0.1.0-canary1.jar` is ACTIVE, not deployed, and runtime untested. Use only the dedicated Matcha Flavoured 26.2 Workbench after a serialized Test Instance Manager assignment; leave the protected 26.1.2 profile untouched. Enable the user's frog-villager resource pack. Do not install the Real Working Villagers reference JAR alongside this candidate. Build/unit results are not Minecraft runtime acceptance.

1. **Shepherd eligibility and action.** Give a shepherd a genuinely claimed loom and place reachable adult unsheared sheep within 12 blocks of it. During normal WORK activity, observe navigation, look, temporary shears, swing, ordinary sheep shear sound/state, and correct colored wool output. Check a baby and an already-sheared adult are ignored; after natural grass regrowth the adult is eligible again. Remove/reassign the loom, move the sheep beyond the local bound, or end WORK time: the custom routine must stop and vanilla villager behavior must resume.
2. **Shepherd ownership and storage.** Drop wool and unrelated items as a player near the sheep; none may be vacuumed. Verify only output from that shepherd's own successful shear enters its owned output. Put barrels on each of the loom's six faces and a separate non-adjacent barrel elsewhere; only face-adjacent barrels may receive wool. Check exact wool color, partial matching stacks before empty slots, component-mismatched stacks kept separate, unrelated contents unchanged, and deterministic choice when multiple adjacent barrels exist. Fill every adjacent barrel: no overwrite, deletion, extraction, rearrangement, or fallback search; wool stays with the villager and shearing stops when safe output capacity is exhausted. Reopen a slot and verify later retry.
3. **Fisherman ownership and cycle.** Give a fisherman a genuinely claimed barrel and reachable open water within eight blocks of it. During WORK, observe bank travel, temporary rod, swing, visible float and line, splash, retrieve, and carrying the catch back. Repeat catches and verify only cod, salmon, tropical fish, or pufferfish appear; no treasure, junk, or fishing XP orbs. Drop unrelated fish/items nearby: they must not be collected. An unclaimed nearby barrel must never receive catch. Check matching and empty slots, component matching, full claimed barrel unchanged, fish retained and new casts paused while output has no safe slot, and retry when space opens. Remove/reassign the claimed barrel or remove the water mid-cast: the routine and float must cancel without adopting another barrel.
4. **Ambient and vanilla regressions.** For Armorer, Butcher/Matcha chef, Cartographer, Cleric, Fletcher, Leatherworker, Librarian, Mason, Toolsmith, and Weaponsmith, verify each uses only its own claimed workstation for occasional look/swing/work sound. Snapshot smoker, blast furnace, brewing stand, cauldron, lectern, and other workstation inventories/blockstates before and after: no item, fuel, fluid, book, map, block, projectile, loot, or resource change is allowed. Farmer and Nitwit remain vanilla. Verify trading, profession acquisition, breeding/social interactions, and normal fence-gate behavior remain intact.
5. **Prop and frog-pack rendering.** While working and after interruption, save/reload, profession change, death, and lightning conversion, check shears/rod never persist or become loot; real carried wool/fish remains available or drops on death/conversion. With the frog pack, inspect head look, 3 px arm swings, shears and rod placement, float and line origin, and workstation gestures at several view angles. Report cosmetic offsets without changing villager geometry.

Stop and record FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE on any resource ownership/storage violation, synthetic tool loot/persistence, workstation side effect, non-fish catch or XP, broken vanilla behavior, renderer conflict, or crash. Record the exact release filename/SHA-256 and observed cases before any acceptance decision.
