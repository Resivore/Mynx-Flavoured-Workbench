# Testing

## Canary 25 — focused small brown toadstool runtime procedure

This procedure is lifecycle-neutral. Record only Minecraft behavior actually observed against the exact `ribbits-private-reconstruction-4.1.6+26.2-mynx-canary25.jar` / SHA-256 `00ff8af7a86d8ea9a0580905e6592e31e362937895d66d5b0aca702247fa6064`. Build, unit tests, private assembly, archive inspection, and resource validation are not Minecraft runtime evidence. Record any separately enabled resource pack because an external override can independently change the visible native huge-brown texture.

1. Confirm `ribbits:small_brown_toadstool`, `ribbits:toadstool`, `ribbits:brown_toadstool`, and `ribbits:red_toadstool` all resolve as four distinct identities. Obtain the new item through Creative or commands and confirm it appears exactly once in Ribbits' ordinary Creative presentation as **Brown Toadstool**.
2. Place the red and brown small toadstools on the same valid and invalid surfaces. Confirm the brown variant follows the red variant's placement restrictions, random positioning, half-block-high selection shape, no-collision behavior, sound, and instant-breaking behavior.
3. Inspect the new inventory sprite and placed block without a separate donor-pack override masking the packaged resources. Confirm the complete cap is brown with no retained red, orange, white, or cream cap spots; the small-toadstool silhouette and stem remain intact; and the existing red small toadstool and huge red/brown blocks remain unchanged.
4. Break the new block normally and confirm it drops itself. Use pick-block and confirm it returns `ribbits:small_brown_toadstool`. Confirm ordinary stacks combine up to the normal block-item limit and never merge with either existing toadstool identity.
5. Confirm both small red and small brown toadstools are accepted by a Composter. The exact `0.65` probability is a static registration contract and must not be inferred from a short random runtime sample; confirm only that the new item participates like the existing small plant and is not treated as the `0.85` huge block.
6. In a clear valid area, bonemeal the brown small toadstool repeatedly. Confirm every produced small toadstool is brown and no red `ribbits:toadstool` appears. Bonemeal the existing red small toadstool separately and confirm its existing red patch remains unchanged.
7. Confirm the new block has no recipe, Toadstool Heart input, Ribbit trade, Wandering Ribbit trade, or other economy presentation. Ordinary natural-generation absence remains primarily a static/reference-scope contract; do not claim it solely from failing to encounter the block during play.

Record `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `INCONCLUSIVE` only for observations actually completed. Stop with `FAIL` on an identity collision, duplicate Creative entry, wrong name, changed placement or shape, collision, wrong drop or pick result, nonstandard stacking, rejected compost input, red output from brown bonemeal, retained red/white cap pixels, changed stem or silhouette, changed native toadstool presentation without an external override, or any recipe, trade, economy, or ordinary-generation exposure.
