## Canary 4 startup failure and Canary 5 repair

The user's startup report binds only to the exact canonical Canary 4 identity recorded in `MYNX_DEVIATIONS.md`: Minecraft aborted during startup with an `InvalidMixinException` because `SwampHutPieceMixin` declared an invalid `@Shadow getWorldPos(int,int,int)` that Mixin could not locate directly on `SwampHutPiece`. It is recorded as an external user-reported runtime `FAIL` for Canary 4 without claiming a managed deployment, occupied test slot, or any Phase C gameplay observation. Canary 5 repaired that defect; Canary 6 and Canary 7 preserve the repair and do not inherit the failure.

Inspection of the exact production-remapped Minecraft 26.2 bytecode found that `getWorldPos` is protected and declared on superclass `net/minecraft/world/level/levelgen/structure/StructurePiece`, not on `SwampHutPiece` or its intermediary superclass. Its runtime name is `getWorldPos`, return type is `net/minecraft/core/BlockPos$MutableBlockPos`, and descriptor is `(III)Lnet/minecraft/core/BlockPos$MutableBlockPos;`. The actual `INVOKEVIRTUAL` instructions in both `SwampHutPiece.postProcess` and `SwampHutPiece.spawnCat` use owner `net/minecraft/world/level/levelgen/structure/structures/SwampHutPiece`, method name `getWorldPos`, and that same descriptor.

Canary 5 removes the invalid shadow, registers a narrow `StructurePiece` invoker for protected `getWorldPos` and the similarly protected `placeBlock`, routes the mixin's direct coordinate transforms and barrel placement through those non-reflective invokers, and targets both `@ModifyArgs` selectors at the real `SwampHutPiece` invocation owner. All four hut hooks and the natural-spawn hook are fail-loud with `require=1` and `allow=1`. The official-namespace production setup does not require a refmap for these selectors, so the absent refmap did not contribute to the Canary 4 failure and adding one would not have repaired the invalid shadow. A production-equivalent Knot/Mixin harness applied `StructurePieceInvoker`, `SwampHutPieceMixin`, and `NaturalSpawnerMixin` successfully and verified each required injection resolved exactly once.

## Phase D Wandering Ribbit

The first attempt is 48,000–72,000 ticks after initialization, a successful visit schedules the next attempt 120,000–168,000 ticks later, a failed attempt retries after 1,200 ticks, and a scheduled visit lasts 48,000 ticks. It holds one global generation-stamped lease, commits it only after the exact event-spawned entity is inserted and all offers materialize, preserves an unloaded holder without force-loading its chunk, lets an active valid trade finish beyond expiry, rejects stale generations on reload, and releases only the exact owned lease on destruction or expiry. Only living Survival or Adventure players in the Overworld are eligible. Stable UUID ordering plus a persisted round-robin cursor prevents permanent first-player bias. Each attempt checks at most 16 positions per selected player and 64 positions total in a 24–48-block annulus, remains at least 16 blocks from every eligible player, uses only already-loaded entity-ready chunks, and rejects invalid height, collision, world-border, fluid, fire, cactus, magma, powder-snow, lit-campfire, or denied-biome sites. The reloadable allow tag is `ribbits:allows_wandering_ribbit_spawns`, the deny tag is `ribbits:without_wandering_ribbit_spawns`, deny wins, and the shipped defaults cover forests/swamp-hut biomes while excluding oceans.

The stable provider boundary sorts unique provider IDs and derives one independent random stream per provider, so an optional provider cannot perturb another provider's selections.
