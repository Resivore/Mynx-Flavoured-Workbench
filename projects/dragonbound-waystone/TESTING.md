# Testing

Canary 11 is statically validated but has not been deployed or runtime tested. Its exact retained artifact is `dragonbound-waystone-0.1.0-canary11.jar`, embedded version `0.1.0-canary11`, 63,380 bytes, SHA-256 `C49AF303BEEF109D9CDFD1444894552AE8C6CD65D419057E9BAFDB691C67C23E`. It preserves Canary 8's body-centered portal ramp (one to four particles per server tick) and 30-particle confirmed-arrival burst. Only the shared player-targeted foreground companion changes: every vanilla `minecraft:portal` particle now starts around `player.position() + (0, 0.05, 0)` in a shallow disk and receives one explicit count-zero packet vector, rather than being spawned near the eye from the look direction.

Canary 10 substantially improved first-person particle density, but the user reports that its eye-forward effect appears around/in front of the camera or from above. That feedback is not a full Canary 10 runtime `PASS`. Canary 11 retains the three-to-eight foreground channel ramp and 24-particle confirmed-arrival burst, with channel starts inside a 0.65-block horizontal radius and arrival starts inside 0.75 blocks; both use Y `0.05 ± 0.05` from the player position. Explicit vectors use a small horizontal drift and positive Y `0.08–0.18`; in Minecraft 26.2's `PortalParticle` first client update, this is an upward launch from the low packet origin. First-person solo-play presentation remains the priority.

The accepted baseline and rollback remain exact `dragonbound-waystone-0.1.0-canary5.jar`, SHA-256 `FF29582CC50F391A3EBB79B5AB1DFF2F8FB7ABB92363BCA46FB8A481AD3`. The exact Canary 4 rollback, SHA-256 `2EA97D762EE646F97954ABC6C919ECEA2F337B91096894ADFC13E5AF34982634`, retains its focused user-reported runtime pass. Canary 6's user-reported `FAIL` remains limited to its pre-arrival audiovisual timing.

## Preconditions

1. Test only under explicit runtime-slot ownership in the dedicated Minecraft 26.2 Fabric Workbench. Never use the protected gameplay instance.
2. Before deployment, verify the exact filename, embedded `dragonbound_waystone` / `0.1.0-canary11` identity, 63,380-byte size, and SHA-256 above.
3. Use Fabric Loader `0.19.3` or newer and Fabric API `0.156.0+26.2` or newer. Dragonbound has no current Matcha Heart or JEI runtime dependency.
4. Preserve the user's world and configuration state. Use disposable state for configuration-migration or destructive loss-protection checks.

## Focused C11 first-person visual matrix

1. While standing on a normal opaque block, perform representative valid Imbued Void Pearl and Dragonbound Staff teleports in first person. From the first channel tick, confirm a few portal particles begin near the player's feet and rise into the lower/central view; density must ramp from three to eight as completion approaches. Look horizontally, upward, and downward, rotate the camera, and move during a channel: the source must remain body/ground-relative rather than orbiting with the view direction, and subsequent particles must follow the new foot position.
2. Confirm the channel effect never reads as rain from above or a stationary eye-level cloud. If convenient, repeat on a slab or other partial-height surface, and try jumping during a cancellable channel; test water only if current channel rules permit it. Do not broaden this canary for unrelated edge cases.
3. For both items, confirm only confirmed arrival emits the brief, stronger 24-particle foreground burst from the destination feet; it must rise through the first-person view and be more pronounced than channeling without lingering. Confirm one Enderman sound remains synchronized with confirmed arrival, Pearl consumption remains success-only, and Staff retains its ordinary one main-hand swing and unchanged success-only cooldown.
4. Cancel channels through movement and accepted damage, and attempt rejected/invalid starts, death, disconnect, dimension/item/slot changes, unsafe state, and obstructed destinations. Confirm body and foreground buildup stop or never begin as appropriate, with no speculative arrival burst, sound, Pearl consumption, or Staff cooldown.
5. In third person, repeat representative Pearl and Staff teleports only to verify no particle spam or pathological duplicate effect; body-centered particles should remain plausible. Preserve the accepted checks for exact destination, unique anchor/loss protection, one-click mouse-release-independent channeling, recipes/discovery, configuration, Staff combat neutrality, corrected four-pixel geometry/UVs, and normal shutdown. Review `latest.log` after normal quit and record only observed behavior.

Stop and record `FAIL` or `INCONCLUSIVE` if particles do not visibly originate near the feet, do not read as rising from below in first person, follow camera look direction, fall from above, remain a stationary eye-level cloud, become an obstruction, produce speculative/missing/duplicated success feedback, regress the UV correction, or change any preserved behavior. Dedicated-server validation remains a separately scheduled scope.
