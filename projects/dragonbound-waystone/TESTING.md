No other Canary 16 failure is inferred. Canary 17 corrects that normal arrival lookup. Canary 18 retains that exact C17 successful-arrival particle/sound presentation for every successful return and, only after teleport, exact-arrival, and held-source-item confirmation, layers Enderscape's own 60-tick default lodestone overlay/vignette (`enderscape:misc/overlay`, `enderscape:misc/vignette`) and its local `enderscape:item.mirror.transdimensional_travel` sound payload for cross-dimension returns. It is statically validated but has not been deployed or runtime tested. Its exact retained artifact is `dragonbound-waystone-0.1.0-canary18.jar`, embedded version `0.1.0-canary18`, 92,060 bytes, SHA-256 `EB33A205B401E91675E0FF635893CFEE943C5E1ACFE658B984A251D3DFB9D0F2`. The accepted release remains exact Canary 12, `dragonbound-waystone-0.1.0-canary12.jar`, SHA-256 `6F621B16652BAF9CAAD89BA923B8792EBC0BA08706A9F507FD17AABFC3121AD6`; do not overwrite it.

Canary 13 has user-reported `RUNTIME_FAIL` evidence limited to selectable-material rendering: a Warped Hyphae-crafted Waystone was malformed in inventory and placed as legacy End Stone Bricks. Canary 14 has a separate user-reported `RUNTIME_FAIL` limited to visual material/UV rendering: a materialized placed Waystone rendered as a huge mosaic of unrelated Minecraft block textures from across the block atlas. The user now reports that the current Waystone/material implementation looks good and works as expected. This binds narrowly to Canary 15's material-rendering work; it is not a general teleport, channel, safety, crafting, or owner-acceptance result. Canary 15 stores and synchronizes only a donor block registry ID; no anchor or destination authority crosses to the client. A component-less or unresolved donor Waystone intentionally uses the accepted End Stone Bricks appearance. Placement retains the exact stack through the existing block entity, so breaking or loss-protected return must retain the selected material too.

## Focused C18 Mirror-arrival checklist

1. Start a valid Pearl and a valid Staff channel, then observe the complete buildup without finishing. Its existing `PORTAL` body stream and foreground rising `REVERSE_PORTAL` stream must begin, ramp, and remain visually/timingly unchanged. Neither must play `enderscape:item.mirror.teleport` or `enderscape:item.mirror.transdimensional_travel`, emit `enderscape:mirror_teleport_in`, or show a Mirror overlay/vignette before the actual teleport.
2. Complete one valid same-dimension return. Only after the player is visibly at the exact Waystone destination and Dragonbound has kept the held source item valid, observe exactly the unchanged C17 Enderscape Mirror arrival punctuation: `enderscape:item.mirror.teleport` and `enderscape:mirror_teleport_in` at the arrival. There must be no crash, transdimensional-travel sound, Mirror overlay/vignette, Enderman teleport sound, vanilla success `PORTAL` burst, success-only foreground `REVERSE_PORTAL` burst, Mirror departure (`mirror_teleport_out`) effect, or advance sound.
3. Cancel a Pearl and Staff channel independently through movement or a held-item change, and attempt one ordinary rejection/obstructed destination where practical. Each path must have no Mirror sound, Mirror arrival particles, overlay, or vignette and must not consume the Pearl or start the Staff cooldown.
4. Complete one valid cross-dimension return when existing configuration permits it. Only after confirmed arrival and held-source-item validation, it must retain the same C17 `enderscape:item.mirror.teleport` and `enderscape:mirror_teleport_in` presentation and add Enderscape's local `enderscape:item.mirror.transdimensional_travel` sound plus its default `enderscape:misc/overlay` and `enderscape:misc/vignette` screen effect for the normal 60-tick duration. Do not accept a departure (`mirror_teleport_out`) effect or any pre-teleport overlay, vignette, or transdimensional sound.
5. For successful Pearl and Staff returns, recheck existing one-Pearl consumption or Staff cooldown, respectively, and the unchanged success message after the complete arrival presentation. The new cross-dimension layer must never make a failed arrival look successful.

Stop and record `FAIL` or `INCONCLUSIVE` if any Mirror effect occurs before confirmation, any failed/cancelled channel produces it, departure choreography appears, same-dimension success gains an interdimensional layer, cross-dimension success lacks its normal C17 arrival presentation or its Enderscape extras, accepted channel buildup changes, or Pearl/Staff semantics regress. Do not claim static validation as a Minecraft runtime PASS.
