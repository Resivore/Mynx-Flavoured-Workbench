Canary 16 has user-reported `RUNTIME_FAIL` evidence limited specifically to its successful-arrival sound lookup crash: it requested nonexistent `enderscape:mirror.teleport` rather than Enderscape 3.0.2's registered `enderscape:item.mirror.teleport`. No other Canary 16 failure is inferred. Canary 17 corrects only that lookup and is statically validated but has not been deployed or runtime tested. Its exact retained artifact is `dragonbound-waystone-0.1.0-canary17.jar`, embedded version `0.1.0-canary17`, 91,328 bytes, SHA-256 `A697300731FE0AE15B01FD0AF6FF5800920FFBF4450A68D31F424FDBF3800DD2`. The accepted release remains exact Canary 12, `dragonbound-waystone-0.1.0-canary12.jar`, SHA-256 `6F621B16652BAF9CAAD89BA923B8792EBC0BA08706A9F507FD17AABFC3121AD6`; do not overwrite it.

Canary 13 has user-reported `RUNTIME_FAIL` evidence limited to selectable-material rendering: a Warped Hyphae-crafted Waystone was malformed in inventory and placed as legacy End Stone Bricks. Canary 14 has a separate user-reported `RUNTIME_FAIL` limited to visual material/UV rendering: a materialized placed Waystone rendered as a huge mosaic of unrelated Minecraft block textures from across the block atlas. The user now reports that the current Waystone/material implementation looks good and works as expected. This binds narrowly to Canary 15's material-rendering work; it is not a general teleport, channel, safety, crafting, or owner-acceptance result. Canary 15 stores and synchronizes only a donor block registry ID; no anchor or destination authority crosses to the client. A component-less or unresolved donor Waystone intentionally uses the accepted End Stone Bricks appearance. Placement retains the exact stack through the existing block entity, so breaking or loss-protected return must retain the selected material too.

Use Fabric Loader `0.19.3` or newer, Fabric API `0.156.0+26.2` or newer, and the required Enderscape `3.0.2` provider from the exact `enderscape-fabric-3.0.2+mc26.2.jar` input, SHA-256 `9FCC4F59CA88E91F90E7C7D18289F2F859F20C810EEBCCA924764AA15236C40B`. ## Focused C17 Mirror-arrival checklist

1. Start a valid Pearl and a valid Staff channel, then observe the complete buildup without finishing. Its existing `PORTAL` body stream and foreground rising `REVERSE_PORTAL` stream must begin, ramp, and remain visually/timingly unchanged. Neither must play `enderscape:item.mirror.teleport` or emit `enderscape:mirror_teleport_in` before the actual teleport.
2. Complete one valid same-dimension return. Only after the player is visibly at the exact Waystone destination and Dragonbound has kept the held source item valid, observe exactly the Enderscape Mirror arrival punctuation: `enderscape:item.mirror.teleport` and `enderscape:mirror_teleport_in` at the arrival. There must be no crash, Enderman teleport sound, vanilla success `PORTAL` burst, success-only foreground `REVERSE_PORTAL` burst, Mirror departure (`mirror_teleport_out`) effect, advance sound, or vignette.
3. Cancel a Pearl and Staff channel independently through movement or a held-item change, and attempt one ordinary rejection/obstructed destination where practical. Each path must have no Mirror sound or Mirror arrival particles and must not consume the Pearl or start the Staff cooldown.
4. Complete one valid cross-dimension return when existing configuration permits it. Its confirmed arrival must use the same `enderscape:item.mirror.teleport` and `enderscape:mirror_teleport_in` presentation as the same-dimension return, without Enderscape's separate transdimensional-travel sound.
5. For successful Pearl and Staff returns, recheck existing one-Pearl consumption or Staff cooldown, respectively, and the unchanged success message after the arrival effect. The new sound/particles must never make a failed arrival look successful.

Stop and record `FAIL` or `INCONCLUSIVE` if the Mirror effect occurs before confirmation, any failed/cancelled channel produces it, departure choreography appears, same- and cross-dimension arrival presentations differ, accepted channel buildup changes, or Pearl/Staff semantics regress. Do not claim static validation as a Minecraft runtime PASS.
